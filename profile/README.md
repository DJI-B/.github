Paper_Tracker is a virtual reality technology company founded in 2025 and headquartered in Shenzhen, China. We focus on VR research, development, and accessory innovation for next-generation immersive experiences.

Our flagship product, the **PaperFaceTracker** series, provides lightweight facial and eye capture modules for VR users. Designed for precision, usability, and seamless integration, PaperFaceTracker brings expressive tracking capabilities to a wider consumer VR audience.

## What We Build

Paper_Tracker develops VR accessory solutions that extend beyond traditional headset ecosystems. Our work focuses on:

- VR facial/eye capture
- VR ecosystem integration

## Our Team

Paper_Tracker was founded by a technical R&D team with experience across mechanical engineering, computer vision, artificial intelligence, embedded systems, and product manufacturing.

We combine product engineering, lean production workflows, and continuous technical exploration to deliver VR products that balance performance, reliability, and value.

## Open Collaboration

As an active contributor to the VR ecosystem, Paper_Tracker supports open collaboration, technical exchange, and partnerships with the global VR technology community.

We believe shared knowledge and community-driven innovation are essential to advancing the future of virtual reality.
